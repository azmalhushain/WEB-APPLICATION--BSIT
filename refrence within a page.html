Computer Hardware

Computer hardware is an umbrella term that refers to the physical components of a computer. Hardware includes tangible items which can be physically touched or seen, and it is required for a computer to function. Computer hardware can be classified as internal or external, which relates to the placement of the physical part in or around the device. Generally, internal hardware is required for the computer to run, while external hardware enhances the use or functionality. Examples of internal computer hardware may include a video card, memory, or a core processing unit (CPU), while external computer hardware examples include a keyboard or a computer case.
